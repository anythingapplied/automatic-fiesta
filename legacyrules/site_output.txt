<< Mission 2 Mission 2 - Rewards Mission 3 >> Dual Suit Stickers FAQs What do we do with banished cards at the end of the mission? Cards from your party that are banished during a mission are gathered up just like the cards in your discard pile - they are not removed from the game. Remember to get all the cards back together (reform your party) before you start working your way through the rewards. << Mission 2 Mission 3 >>

<< Mission 2 Mission 3 Rewards >> Mission 3 Rules FAQs There's a jester in the mission zone... now what? In a game with more than one jester (i.e. a 4-player game), a jester in the mission zone does NOT prevent other jesters from being played and applying their rule.
                                    The jester is not a class ability but rather a special rule that triggers when a Jester is played. A jester in the mission zone has no effect - lucky you! << Mission 2 Rewards >>

<< Mission 6 Mission 7 Mission 8 >> Mission 7 Rules Druids FAQs Pilgrims can't leave my hand, really? Yes really! But it's just a special rule for this mission only. If you bump into any pilgrims in future missions you will be free to play or discard them like any other card. Can I 'feign death' with a pilgrim in my hand? No! You must discard your whole hand in order to feign death so if a pilgrim is in your hand it isn't possible to feign death.
                                    Be very careful with those pilgrims for this mission! << Mission 6 Mission 8 >>

<< Mission 7 Mission 8 Mission 9 >> Mission 8 Rules Chanters FAQs Can I play pilgrims? Yes! Pilgrims are like any other card. The restriction that prevented them from leaving your hand was just a special mission rule for Mission 7. You can play them or discard them to damage as normal for mission 8 onwards. As a solo player, how does the Kinfolk Flute work with the new Chanters? "Any time" is literally whenever you want.. even in the middle of resolving a class ability - so yes, you can draw a whole bunch of cards with the chanter, throw one on the relic and then discard down to hand size. << Mission 7 Mission 9 >>
